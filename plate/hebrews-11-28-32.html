An oil painting in the tradition of the old masters — for this passage of Scripture.

By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of the blood, that the destroyer of the firstborn should not touch them.

By faith they passed through the Red Sea as on dry land.

When the Egyptians tried to do so, they were swallowed up.

By faith the walls of Jericho fell down after they had been encircled for seven days.

By faith Rahab the prostitute didn’t perish with those who were disobedient, having received the spies in peace.

What more shall I say?

For the time would fail me if I told of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets—
