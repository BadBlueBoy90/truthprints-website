Psalm 33:1-7

Psalms · The Illustrated Bible · TruthPrints
An oil painting in the tradition of the old masters — for this passage of Scripture.

Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous!

Praise is fitting for the upright.

Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre.

Sing praises to him with the harp of ten strings.

Sing to him a new song.

Play skillfully with a shout of joy!

For the LORD’s word is right.

All his work is done in faithfulness.

He loves righteousness and justice.

The earth is full of the loving kindness of the LORD.

By the LORD’s word, the heavens were made: all their army by the breath of his mouth.

He gathers the waters of the sea together as a heap.

He lays up the deeps in storehouses.
