Jeremiah 9:8-10

Jeremiah · The Illustrated Bible · TruthPrints
An oil painting in the tradition of the old masters — for this passage of Scripture.

Their tongue is a deadly arrow.

It speaks deceit.

One speaks peaceably to his neighbor with his mouth, but in his heart, he waits to ambush him.

Shouldn’t I punish them for these things?”

says the LORD.

“Shouldn’t my soul be avenged on a nation such as this?

I will weep and wail for the mountains, and lament for the pastures of the wilderness, because they are burned up, so that no one passes through; Men can’t hear the voice of the livestock.

Both the birds of the sky and the animals have fled.

They are gone.
